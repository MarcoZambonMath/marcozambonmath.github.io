Differentiable manifolds

Marco Zambon, University of Porto

Fall 2009

This is a first semester course in the joint PhD program between the Universities of Coimbra and Porto.

Instructor: Dr. Marco Zambon (mzambon@fc.up.pt)

Classes: Mondays 11h-13h in Sala 2.4 and 14h30-16h30 in Sala 3.10 of the mathematics department of the Univ. of Coimbra.

Textbook: For most of the course I will follow the textbook "An Introduction To Differential Manifolds" by Dennis Barden & Charles Thomas (Imperial College Press).

Homework: Every two weeks you will have to hand in solutions to homework problems and we will discuss them.
