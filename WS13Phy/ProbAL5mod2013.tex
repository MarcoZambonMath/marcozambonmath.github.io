   \n Resolver los siguientes
    sistemas de ecuaciones:
    $$
    a)\left\{\begin{array}{rrr}
      x+2y-z &= & 7 \\
      2x+y+z &= & 6 \\
      x-y+3z &= & -1
    \end{array}
    \right.\hspace{5mm}b)\left\{\begin{array}{rrr}
      2x+3y-5z-2 &= & 0 \\
      3x-y+2z+1 &= & 0 \\
      5x+4y-6z-3 &= & 0
    \end{array}
    \right.\hspace{5mm}c)\left\{\begin{array}{rrr}
      2x+y+4z+8t &= & -1 \\
      x+3y-6z+2t &= & 3 \\
      3x-2y+2z-2t &= & 8 \\
      2x-y+2z &= & 4
    \end{array}
    \right.
    $$

    o aplicando el método de Gauss.
