Ciencia sem fronteiras

This is the website of the Pesquisador Visitante Especial project number 88881.030367/2013-01, awared as part of the Ciencia sem fronteiras program (Project call n. 71/2013 - third chronogram).

Project:
  • Title of the project: HOMOTOPY MOMENT MAPS, DEFORMATION THEORY, SINGULAR FOLIATIONS AND POISSON TOPOLOGY
  • Principal Investigator: Marco Zambon ( KU Leuven, Belgium)
  • Technical coordinator: Eduardo Hoefel (UFPR, Curitiba)
  • Main location: Universidade Federal do Parana' (UFPR) in Curitiba, Brazil.
  • Duration of the project: from March 1, 2014 to March 1, 2017

Project team: The project team consist of more than 20 researchers based both in Brazil and Europe.
  • List of PROJECT TEAM MEMBERS, with links to their CVs, contact information, and personal websites.
  • List of INSTITUTIONS participating in the project.
  • Postdocs associated to the project: Alexandre Quesney and Luiz Henrique Pereira Pegas (UFPR).

Funding: The project provides funds for
  • project members to cover travel and local expenses:
    • during their visits to Curitiba and to the principal investigator's home institution, KU Leuven (Belgium).
    • during their visits to other project members' institutions.
  • postdoc positions in Curitiba for a total of 36 monhts. They can be broken down into several shorter postdocs.
  • the principal Investigator to visit regularly Curitiba.
Funding request: please contact Eduardo Hoefel or Marco Zambon.
Acknowledgements: in the publications that arose from the funding of this project, please mention a phrase like "...partially supported by Projeto P.V.E. 88881.030367/2013-01 (CAPES/Brazil)".
